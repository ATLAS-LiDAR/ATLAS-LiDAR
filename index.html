ATLAS-Sandbox · v2

The ATLAS attack, visualized

ATLAS generates adversarial LiDAR data offline: it perturbs recorded frames and hands the result to a detector. What the perturbation actually is — which returns die, where a phantom vehicle comes from, why a given point survives — stays buried in the pipeline. This is that pipeline, running in front of you, one frame at a time.

Everything here is a port of ATLAS's own algorithms. Injection samples a real recorded vehicle from the trace library and places it ahead of the ego; removal aims a roadside A-HFR spoofer at a vehicle you pick and deletes returns inside its sector. Watch the sensor spin and the cloud fill in the order it was really acquired.
