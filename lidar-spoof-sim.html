A pulse-level LiDAR spoofing sandbox

Raw-signal spoofing attacks are not arbitrary edits to a point cloud — an attacker can only touch the beams its laser can physically reach, and only within the sensor's scanning geometry. This figure makes that constraint tangible. Place the ego sensor and the spoofer, pick a LiDAR, and shape the attack; the sensor ray-casts a static scene beam by beam while the spoofer injects or removes returns strictly inside its reachable window.

Compare the clean ground-truth return against the perturbed return in real time, and toggle scan randomization to watch a synchronized attack collapse.
